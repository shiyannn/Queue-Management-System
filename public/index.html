Standard Operating Procedure

Clinic Queue Workflow

Designed for orderly and efficient patient service across all consulting rooms.

Phase 01

Patient Arrival & Triage

Patients are greeted at reception or triage upon arrival. Staff issues their designated queue number based on medical or dental service requirements.

Phase 02

Waiting Area Audio-Visual Call

Patients wait comfortably in the designated lobby area. The public TV monitor automatically sounds a chime and announces the number when a doctor is ready.

Phase 03

Doctor Consultation & Routing

Physicians manage patient flow directly from their room terminal. Counters advance sequentially, with options to recall, hold, or transfer patients.
